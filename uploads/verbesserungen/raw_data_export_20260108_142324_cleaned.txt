## 1. Redesigned Process Flow

- All warranty requests are submitted via a digital self-service portal, which automatically registers the request and captures all necessary information upfront.
- Upon registration, the workflow management system checks ticket validity automatically.
    - If the ticket is invalid, the process ends with an automated rejection notification to the customer.
    - If valid, the system applies triage:
        - Simple, low-risk cases are routed for "casual examination" by available qualified resources (excluding Sara and Sean for compliance).
        - Complex or ambiguous cases are routed for "thorough examination" exclusively by Sean.
- After examination:
    - The workflow system automatically assigns the case to Sara for decision-making, ensuring compliance.
    - Sara reviews the examination outcome and selects either "pay compensation" or "reject request." If further clarification is needed, the case is re-routed for additional examination (casual or thorough, as per triage).
- All customer communications (status updates, requests for additional information, decision notifications) are consolidated and managed through the portal, reducing manual follow-ups and touchpoints.
- The workflow system logs all actions, timestamps, and resource assignments for monitoring and further optimization.

## 2. Major changes

- Introduction of a digital self-service portal for request registration and communication.
- Automated ticket validity checks, eliminating manual pre-checks.
- Application of triage to route cases directly to either casual or thorough examination based on case complexity.
- Strict digital enforcement of compliance restrictions via workflow automation (e.g., only Sara can decide, only Sean can perform thorough investigations).
- Consolidation of all customer interactions into a single digital channel, reducing delays and manual work.
- Removal of unnecessary handovers and rework loops by ensuring clear routing and information completeness upfront.
