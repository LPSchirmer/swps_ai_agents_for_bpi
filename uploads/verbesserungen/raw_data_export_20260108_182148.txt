## 1. Redesigned Process Flow
- All warranty requests are registered by the customer via a digital self-service portal, eliminating manual intake and reducing administrative workload.
- Upon registration, the system automatically checks ticket validity and completeness using rule-based automation; incomplete or invalid tickets are auto-rejected, requiring no human intervention.
- Valid tickets are triaged by the system:
  - *Simple/standard cases* are routed to "examine casually" by available resources (excluding decision-makers).
  - *Complex/exceptional cases* are routed directly to "examine thoroughly" (assigned to Sean as per compliance).
- After examination, cases are forwarded to Sara, the sole decision-maker, via workflow automation.
- Sara reviews only the system-prepared case summary and makes the compensation/rejection decision.
- If compensation is approved, the system triggers automated payment processing; if rejected, the customer is notified automatically.
- All process steps are logged and monitored digitally, with no manual reinitiation loops permitted; instead, unclear or incomplete cases are auto-closed by the system with a prompt for resubmission.
- All documentation and communication are managed through the digital portal, eliminating redundant email and paper flows.

## 2. Major changes
- Introduction of a digital self-service portal for request intake and status tracking.
- Automated ticket validation and triage, reducing manual checking and resource usage.
- Elimination of manual reinitiation and unnecessary process loops through strict digital workflow enforcement.
- Standardization of process variants: only two main paths (simple vs. complex) remain, reducing coordination and resource costs.
- Automated communication and documentation management, removing redundant administrative activities.

## 3. Used process redesign patterns
1. Apply Integral Technology (Digital self-service portal, automation of ticket checks, workflow management)

- If additional information is needed, the system prompts the customer directly via the portal, minimizing manual reinitiation and communication loops.
- Once Sara makes a decision:
    - If compensation is approved, payment is triggered automatically.
    - If rejected, the customer is notified immediately via the portal.
- All process steps are handled case-by-case (no batching), and progress is visible to all involved resources in real-time dashboards.
- The workflow system logs all actions, enabling process mining and continuous optimization.
