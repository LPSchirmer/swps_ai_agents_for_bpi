Customer Heuristics 
- Control relocation: Move controls towards the customer 
- Contact reduction: Reduce the number of contacts with customers and third parties 
- Integration: Consider the integration with a business process of the customer/supplier 
Business Process Operation Heuristics 
- Case types: Determine whether activities are related to the same type of case  
- Activity elimination: Eliminate unnecessary activities from a business process 
- Case-based work: Remove batch-processing and periodic activities 
- Triage: Split an activity into alternative versions  
- Activity composition: Combine small activities into composite activities 
Business Process Behaviour Heuristics 
- Resequencing: Move activities to their appropriate place  
- Parallelism: Put activities in parallel  
- Knock-out: Order knock-outs in an increasing order of effort  
- Exception: Design business processes for typical cases and isolate exceptional cases 
Information Heuristics 
- Control addition: Check the completeness and correctness of incoming materials 
- Buffering: Buffer information from an external source and subscribe to updates 
Organization Heuristics 
- Case assignment: Let participants perform as many steps as possible 
- Flexible assignment: Keep generic participants free for as long as possible  
- Centralization: Let geographically dispersed participants act as if they are centralized 
- Split responsibilities: Avoid shared responsibilities for tasks from different units  
- Customer teams: Consider composing work teams of people from different departments  
- Numerical involvement: Minimize the number of involved departments, groups and persons  
- Case manager: Appoint one person to be responsible for the handling of each type of case 
- Extra resources: If capacity is insufficient, increase the available number of resources  
- Specialize: Consider deepening the skills of participants  
- Empower: Give workers decision-making authority instead of relying on middle management 
Technology Heuristics 
- Activity automation: Consider automating activities 
- Integral technology: Elevate physical constraints in a business process by applying new technology  
External Environment Heuristics 
- Interfacing: Consider a standardized interface with customers and partners  
- Outsourcing: Consider outsourcing a business process completely or parts of it  
- Trusted party: Use the insights of a trusted party
