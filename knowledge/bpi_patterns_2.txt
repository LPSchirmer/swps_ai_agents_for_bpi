- Eliminate: remove non-value-added activities 
- Replace: replace an activity with a more suitable activity 
- Insert: Insert a new activity into a process 
- Improve: Improve the way a single activity is performed. 
- Delay: delay activities to gain time and avoid early individualization 
- Pull instead of Push: Introduction of the pull principle 
- Resequence: change the sequence of activities 
- Consolidate: merge several activities into one 
- Individualize: split an activity into several alternatives 
- Decouple (horizontal): split an activity into two successive activities 
- Decouple (vertical): split an activity into two parallel activities 
- Integration: merge of two successive activities 
- Specialize: create two (or more) variants of activities 
- Natural Order: parallelize two previously sequential activities 
- Optional: Set an activity as optional
